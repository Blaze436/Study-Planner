# Paper System

## What is Paper System?
**It the Paper version of this app that I have used for four years. I designed it to help me plan huge Goals by breaking them into small manageable steps that I was capable of taking everyday until the Goal was complete.**

## How does it work?
**It took a lot of initial effort. First, I had to break down the huge Goal into small and equal steps. Then, I setup the layout: On my older versions it was simple with S.no, Date, Subject(name of the task), Description and a tick-off in this same exact order. On my later version like the Cycle, I had to also mention Cycle, order and number of blocks done/remaing manually. After which I added the Blocks and did them alike.**


## Why is the app not the Cycle version?

 **While, it may be tempting to create the app around the cycle version I think we shouldn't: Firstly, for small tasks it is an overkill. Secondly, it was the best version that I created not that anyone else did, meaning it is possible for much better alternatives to exist. This app is the foundation, the user's imagination shoud be the ceiling.**

## Questions:


1. What are the units on paper? (goal, cycle, block, day?) Does a block carry a date?

     Yes, It does a carry a Date but not so simply. Before, a date(required) was assigned to a block, later in cycle version, a block/s was assigned to a date. The units were S.no, Date, Subject, Description and Tick-Off.

---

2. How long is a cycle, and how many blocks go in one? How are they ordered?

     A cycle can last however long you want it to, I used a week as a cycle. Depends on however many you want, multiple per day is ideal for buffering and stuff. The blocks are in chronological order, though, they can be arranged however you like. One important thing a cycle can Span multiple Goals like 7 subjects -> 7 Goals -> but 1 cycle. In this case the blocks are chronological by Goal and the minimum number of blocks can vary by each goal for the cycle.

---

3. How do you decide what to do today, step by step?

     I wake up look at the task at hand for today and commit, if after I have done the minimum and I still want to do more I do tommorrows.

---

4. You miss a block on a given day. What physically happens, step by step?

     Incase a block was missed: Before, I moved it to the next day or used a buffer block if available. Later in the cycle version, the blocks missed were distribute equaly to the future, the order of the blocks remained, and the minimum of upcoming days increased.

--- 

5. You finish extra blocks. What happens?

     You benefit by gaining buffer days or potentially finishing early. In the notebook I added +/- with a small number that got carried over each days: If it was negative I am behind and if it was positive I am ahead. I had to do some mental math each time to get a proper idea of my standing.

---

6. A cycle ends with unfinished blocks. What happens on planning day?
     
      Firstly, you have to mess up real bad to not meet the minimum for many days consecutively, it never really happened to me. But if it did, those blocks would get evenly distributed to the next cycle, increasing the minimum and I would personally evaluate the reason to point out any fundamental problem with the block/s in question.

---

7. How far ahead do you plan?

      When I first started planning I used to plan months ahead but obviously circumsatnces and situations change, hence, I could never fully complete. Eventually I learned that futher you planned the less accurate you got, all you had to do was just create blocks and plan them cycle by cycle(week by week) as planning a week into the future holds much more certainty than a month. Hence, I designated a special planning day at the end of each cycle that helped me review, evaluate and plan.

---

8. How do you track progress?

     Pages upon pages for larger Goals like completing my syllabus. I had a Tick-Off for every task and a rough pencil line for the minimum. Ticking away was the progress, It was visually simple.

---

9. What do you do when a goal changes or is dropped?

      The Goals exist seperately, independent of each other. Working independentaly, one doesn't affect the other. A change only happens to the Goal and, depending on the change, the plan changes accordingly, but no other Goals are affected. Same for when a Goal is dropped. I just abandon or cross out the pages.

---

10. What does paper do for you that you never noticed?

     Physical existant and the sheer manual time you initialy put in was what made it special. The paper version was personal and mine which added a lot that no other to-do apps could. This feeling of personal creation I want to re-create by giving the user a platform to create and expand on themselves.
